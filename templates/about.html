Reading the pages

Findings sets out what the data shows statewide. Schools and Counties make up the Atlas: a map of every measure, and a profile page for each of the 244 school systems and 93 counties.

Each profile leads with eight key measures. The diamond on each tile places it among all systems or all counties, lowest at the left; the tick marks the median. Hover over or tab to a measure's name for its definition.

Every change carries a sign. Money over time is in 2019 dollars unless the label says otherwise.
