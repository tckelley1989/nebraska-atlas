Real weekly wages changed {{ f.wage_rural }} in rural counties and {{ f.wage_metro }} in metro counties from 2019 to the latest four quarters. Real income per person changed {{ f.inc_rural }} in rural counties and {{ f.inc_metro }} in metro counties from 2019 to 2024.

The two measures cover different things. Wages count payroll jobs only, which leave out the self-employed. Income per person also counts farm income, transfers and investment income, counted where people live.
