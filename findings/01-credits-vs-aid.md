From 2019-20 to {{ f.money_year }}, state-funded relief on school property taxes rose {{ f.relief_gain }}, while TEEOSA aid received rose {{ f.teeosa_gain }} (current dollars). Relief here counts property tax credits paid to districts and the LB 1107 school credit in both years, whether owners claimed it on income tax returns or it reached districts as a credit.

<!-- more -->

Counting only credits booked in districts' General Funds, the rise looks larger, {{ f.credits_gain }}, because in 2019-20 the LB 1107 credit ({{ f.lb1107_0 }}) was claimed on income tax returns and never reached district books.

Credits are a share of taxes levied, and taxes levied are taxable valuation times the levy, so credits are largest where taxable valuation per student is highest. Equalization aid works the other way: it falls as school adjusted valuation per student rises.
