From 2019-20 to {{ f.money_year }}, property tax credits paid into districts' General Funds rose {{ f.credits_gain }}, while TEEOSA aid rose {{ f.aid_gain }} (current dollars).

Credits are a share of taxes levied, and taxes levied are taxable value times the levy, so credits are largest where property wealth is. Equalization aid works the other way: it falls as property wealth per student rises.
